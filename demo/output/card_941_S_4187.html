Final = clinical log-probabilities + KAN correction (K2 residual, mean of 25 KANs). Total correction to each logit: CN -1.37, EMCI +0.65, LMCI +0.08, AD -1.50.

MRI CN logit → AD logit (decreasing in all 25 + 25 models): input -0.02, contribution +0.01 to the AD logit.

MRI EMCI logit → LMCI logit (decreasing in all 25 + 25 models): input +0.46, contribution -0.02 to the LMCI logit.

Only MRI edges monotone in the same direction in all 25 val-stage and all 25 frozen KANs are shown; the rest of the correction comes from the other edges (age, CDR-SB, CogIndex, MMSE, clinical logits, and MRI edges without a consistent shape, including MRI AD → AD). MRI logits are centred and sum to zero, so their edges are read jointly.

No scan: MRI inputs are the neutral values, so the MRI edges contribute their neutral-point value.
