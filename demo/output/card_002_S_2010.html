Only MRI edges monotone in the same direction in all 25 val-stage and all 25 frozen KANs are shown; the rest of the correction comes from the other edges (age, CDR-SB, CogIndex, MMSE, clinical logits, and MRI edges without a consistent shape, including MRI AD → AD).
